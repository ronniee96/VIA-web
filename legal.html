Terms & Conditions of Engagement

Commercial & Technical Execution Terms for Industrial Services

By accessing this website, requesting a quotation, or contracting Vidarbha Industrial Associates for high-pressure hydro-jetting, chemical circulation descaling, retubing, or NDT inspection, your organization agrees to these Terms & Conditions.

2.1 Quotation & Mobilization Basis

All formal commercial quotations generated via our online portal or dispatch hotline are estimates subject to formal technical review by our Managing Director P.C. Mitra. Final contracts require formal purchase order (PO) issuance and mutually signed Work Orders defining:

  • Equipment scope (e.g., number of boiler tubes, condenser dimensions, scale composition).
  • Required pressure tier (150 BAR light wash up to 1100 BAR heavy scale cutting).
  • Mobilization timetable and emergency mobilization fees where 2-hour response is invoked.

2.2 Client Plant Site Obligations

To ensure safety and seamless turnaround execution, client facilities must furnish:

  • Valid Permit-to-Work (PTW), Confined Space Entry permits, and Lock-Out/Tag-Out (LOTO) clearances before jetting commencement.
  • Continuous clean industrial water supply at specified volume (minimum 40–180 LPM depending on pump rating).
  • Adequate 3-phase industrial power supply (or approved diesel fuel provisions for trailer rigs).
  • Safe effluent disposal points compliant with local State Pollution Control Board (MPCB/CECB) guidelines.

2.3 Payment Terms & Statutory Invoicing

Invoices are issued in accordance with GST rules under GSTIN 27AHTPM2150M1ZU. Standard commercial terms entail a mobilization advance for outstation deployments, with milestone or final clearance payments within the agreed credit window upon submission of joint technical inspection and clearance certificates.

2.4 Safety & Statutory Compliance

VIA maintains full compliance with the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act, Employee State Insurance (ESIC), Employee Provident Fund (EPF), and Workman's Compensation. All jetting technicians wear certified Kevlar/Dyneema ballistic suits and operate with safety dump foot control valves.

2.5 Limitation of Liability & Force Majeure

VIA is not liable for indirect, consequential, or operational downtime losses arising from pre-existing metallurgical embrittlement, stress corrosion cracking, or undisclosed structural degradation of client equipment. Neither party is liable for delays due to acts of God, extreme natural disasters, or statutory plant shutdowns beyond reasonable control.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

B2B Heavy Industrial Service Mobilization & Quality Guarantee

Because Vidarbha Industrial Associates provides specialized, capital-intensive heavy mechanical engineering services involving heavy trailer pump mobilization, specialized chemical reagents, and dedicated crew deployment, our refund and cancellation policies reflect standard Indian B2B engineering practices.

4.1 Turnaround & Satisfaction Guarantee ("We Work Until Done Right")

In keeping with the founding motto of Managing Director P.C. Mitra, we guarantee that all contracted hydro-jetting and chemical cleaning meets or exceeds mutually specified technical cleanliness parameters (e.g., NACE/SSPC visual standards or endoscopy tube inspection). If joint inspection demonstrates residual scale attributable to our process, we will re-jet and clean without additional service labor charge until the tube sheet/condenser passes quality inspection.

4.2 Mobilization Cancellations

  • Cancellation prior to equipment departure: If a client cancels a scheduled maintenance engagement more than 48 hours prior to equipment dispatch from our Chandrapur yard, any advance payment will be refunded after deducting documented administrative/scaffolding reservation fees (maximum 10%).
  • Cancellation after machinery transit: Once high-pressure pumps (1100 BAR trailer rigs) and crews have been dispatched, the mobilization fee is non-refundable, as it covers specialized transport, diesel freight, and crew staging costs.
  • Client Plant Readiness Delays: If work cannot commence due to unreadied client permits, lack of water supply, or plant access holds, standard demurrage/idle crew rates agreed in the Work Order will apply.

4.3 Refund Processing Timelines

Approved commercial refunds are processed via RTGS/NEFT to the original issuing corporate bank account within 7 to 14 business days, accompanied by a formal credit note.
