28 Years of Heavy Industrial Cleaning Mastery

Headquartered in Chandrapur, Maharashtra — the epicentre of central India’s thermal power generation, coal mining, and heavy steel production — Vidarbha Industrial Associates (VIA) was founded in 1997 to solve the toughest fouling and heat-transfer challenges in heavy industry.

Under the leadership of veteran director P.C. Mitra and senior mechanical engineers, VIA operates a battle-tested fleet of 15 high-pressure units capable of generating pressures up to 1100 BAR. Proven Plant Case Study

210 MW Thermal Station Condenser Turnaround

At a major thermal power station, severe silica and calcium scale had choked over 4,200 condenser tubes, causing excessive turbine exhaust backpressure and risking a 60 MW generation curtailment.

The VIA Two-Step Synergistic Method

STEP 1
Inhibited Chemical Circulation: Softens dense silica matrix without etching underlying parent tube metal.
STEP 2
1100 BAR High-Velocity Lancing: Dislodges softened scale plugs and ejects them cleanly from tube bore.
